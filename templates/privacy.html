Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expenses you log, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

We do not collect information you have not entered, and we do not track your location.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to run the app: to sign you in, to save your expenses, and to show you totals, category breakdowns and summaries for the periods you choose.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including keeping passwords in hashed form. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the associated records at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy.

We do not share your expense records with third parties. If we add other services in future, this policy will be updated to say so.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details published on the Spendly website.
